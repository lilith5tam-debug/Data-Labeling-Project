## 🎙️ Project 1: Multi-Dialect Arabic Audio Annotation & Phonetics (Expert Tier)
This high-difficulty project leverages deep linguistic expertise to curate and transcribe dialect-specific data to train Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) AI models, ensuring strict separation of distinct regional sub-dialects.

### 🔍 Demo Annotation Samples:
* 📜 **Modern Standard Arabic (Fusha):** High-fidelity poetry transcription with precise phonetics and full diacritics (*تشكيل إعرابي كامل للمحافظة على الوزن والقافية*).
* 🇸🇾 **Syrian Arabic (Aleppine Dialect):** Capturing deep emotional semantics, conversational de-escalation, and distinct vocabulary like (*اشبك خاي, ماو كويس طاول*).
* 🏙️ **Syrian Arabic (Damascene Dialect):** Mapping high-inflection sarcasm, defiant refusal tone, and precise local vocalization such as (*إي لائة!, ما حزرت بنوب*).

*📂 Output File:* `arabic_audio_labels.json` (Structured speech-to-text data mapping)
